## 2. PCB Vibration Research — Danish
Look into how vibrations during rocket flight might affect our PCBs, sensors, connectors and other electronics.

### A. Vibration Effects & Forces (Danish + Sivi)

- [ ] Research where vibrations come from during flight (motor, airflow, parachute deployment, etc.).
- [ ] Understand how vibration frequency, amplitude, resonance and acceleration affect PCBs.
- [ ] Identify possible problems like PCB bending, loose connectors, damaged solder joints and intermittent connections.
- [ ] Look into ways to estimate vibration forces using calculations, flight simulation or ANSYS.

### B. PCB Mounting Solutions —(Danish + Sivi / Structures)

- [ ] Research different PCB mounting methods (rigid mounts, standoffs, rubber dampers, vibration isolators, etc.).
- [ ] Compare their pros and cons in terms of vibration protection, weight, cost and practicality.
- [ ] Look into how PCB position, orientation and enclosure design affect vibration.
- [ ] Suggest a suitable mounting solution for our rocket and explain why.

### C. Hardware Validation & Lab Testing (Danish + Sivi)

- [ ] Research how we can test PCB vibrations in the lab (shaker table, vibration sweep, random vibration, shock testing, etc.).
- [ ] Look into using accelerometers to measure PCB vibration and resonance.
- [ ] Suggest realistic experiments we can carry out using available university equipment or low-cost setups.
- [ ] Decide what we should measure to check whether the PCB mounting works (vibration response, loose parts, data loss, connection issues, etc.).
- [ ] Explore how we could compare different mounting solutions through testing.
